About A & M Racing

A & M Racing Team is fueled by two young drivers with big talent and even bigger heart — Axel (12) and Maci (9). This fast‑moving duo brings the heat every time they roll onto the track. Backing them is the ultimate support squad: Kelli and Thomas — Mom and Dad — the powerhouse pit crew who keep this team running strong on and off the course. Papa Bert takes command as Crew Chief, dialing in every detail to keep the kids race‑ready, while Granny handles team hospitality like a seasoned pro, keeping everyone energized and in winning form. And holding down the role of morale captain is Sage, the legendary racing kitty — provider of luck, cuddles, and championship‑level vibes at every event.
